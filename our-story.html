The Hands Behind the Crust

Rising with the embers of the pre-dawn hearth

Long before the first morning light breaks through the district lanes, our ovens are already warm. Founded by a small collective of passionate home bakers turned professional artisans, Hearth & Rise Sourdough was born from a singular obsession with three elements: wild yeast, stone-ground flour, and live wood fire.

We believe that great bread cannot be rushed. It requires a quiet patience, an understanding of the day's humidity, and a deep respect for the natural fermentation process. Our mission is to return to the historical roots of baking, delivering a crust that crackles and a crumb that nourishes.

Our Artisanal Craft

The 48-Hour Slow Fermentation

Our bread takes time. We slow-mature every batch over two full days to naturally unlock deep complexities of flavor, texture, and digestibility.

01

Locally Milled Stone Grains

We partner with regional heritage mills to source raw, pesticide-free grains. Freshly milled, these stone-ground flours retain the essential germ, oils, and wild yeasts that store-bought flour leaves behind.

The Foundation
02

48-Hour Cold Cure

Our sourdough undergoes a patient, low-temperature rise. Over 48 hours, lactobacilli digest complex sugars, cultivating our signature subtle tang and exceptionally light, open crumb.

The Development
03

The Wood-Fired Bake

Cooked inside a blazing hearth at intense heat, steam-injected chambers allow the crust to expand to its maximum potential, finalizing in a deeply caramelized, beautifully blistered ear.
