Our 36-Hour Rhythm

The Slow Fermentation

Time is our most critical ingredient. Step inside the humid, amber warmth of our dough room where natural wild cultures do their slow work overnight.

01
Day 1 — 06:00 AM

The Milling & Feeding

We stone-grind ancient rye and heirloom wheat varieties in-house, immediately hydrating the flour to awaken the natural wheat enzymes.

02
Day 1 — 02:00 PM

The Gentle Autolyse

No machines, no harsh mixing. The dough is pulled and folded by hand every half hour, creating strong gluten bonds while preserving native wheat flavors.

03
Day 1 — 08:00 PM

The Cold Retardation

Shaped loaves rest in traditional wicker bannetons inside our cool proofing cellar. Overnight, wild yeasts develop complex lactic and acetic acids.

04
Day 2 — 05:00 AM

The Stone Bake

Scored with razor blades and loaded onto our intense hearth, steam is injected immediately to gelatinize surface starches for a blistered, caramelized crust.
