The Hearth Philosophy

Three Ingredients, Handled with Conviction.

Flour, water, and salt. We do not rush the wild leaven. Our classic loaves ferment naturally for thirty hours, coaxing rich organic acids and sugars to the surface to form a beautifully blistered, digestible sourdough.

The resulting bread has a custard-like interior crumb wrapped tightly inside a deeply caramelized, smoke-infused wood-fired shell.

The Pre-Dawn Process

Fermented in the Dark

We structure our bakery steps around the silent fermentation cycle. Step inside the glow of the oven room.

01
Wld

Wild Culture

Our starter is kept healthy with pure mountain spring water and stoneground rye. It yields the complex organic acids that give our bread its distinct, subtle tang.

02
30h

Slow Ferment

Over a thirty-hour cold prove, the wild yeast slowly consumes complex sugars, making the final crumb tender and much easier on human digestion.

03
Fire

Hearth Bake

We peel loaves directly onto our heavy firebrick deck. Extreme heat generates rapid oven spring and creates a crisp, deeply blistered crust.
